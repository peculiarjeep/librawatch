How flagging works

Thresholds are configurable in Settings

2+ failed logins

Any student ID with this many rejected sign-ins in the log is listed here automatically.

3 attempts within 10 min

A rapid cluster of failures is treated as credential guessing and raised to Critical.

Severity from attempt volume

4 or more failures is High, 3 is Medium, and the threshold value itself is Low.

Credentials are never read

Only student ID, date, result, device and time in/out columns are imported. Password columns stay untouched and are never displayed.
